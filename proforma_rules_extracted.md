## Observation Structure Patterns

### Surveillance Commencement
Pattern: "Surveillance commenced in the vicinity of [ADDRESS] ([BRACKETED_ADDRESS])."
- Must begin with "Surveillance commenced in the vicinity of"
- Full address followed by bracketed short address in parentheses
- Ends with a full stop

### Vehicle Observation — Departure
Pattern: "A [COLOUR] [MAKE] [MODEL] bearing [STATE] registration [REGO] (Vehicle [REGO]) with [OCCUPANT_DESC], departed from [ADDRESS] ([BRACKETED_ADDRESS]), travelled on [ROAD] and continued [out of sight / via:]."
- Vehicle always identified as "Vehicle [REGO]" in parentheses on first mention
- Occupants described as "unseen occupants" or named with role
- Departure uses "departed from [ADDRESS]"
- Route listed as comma-separated street names ending with suburb and "whereat;" when multiple streets
- Ends with "continued out of sight" or "continued via:" followed by route list

### Vehicle Observation — Travel
Pattern: "Vehicle [REGO], with [PERSON] as driver and [ROLE], travelled on [ROAD], [SUBURB] and continued [out of sight / via:]."
- After first mention, vehicle referred to as "Vehicle [REGO]" (no colour/make/model)
- Driver identified as "[NAME] as driver and sole occupant" or "[NAME] as driver and [ROLE]"
- Travel uses "travelled on [ROAD]"

### Vehicle Observation — Parking
Pattern: "Vehicle [REGO], with [PERSON] as driver and [ROLE], [parked / verge parked] at [ADDRESS] ([BRACKETED_ADDRESS])."
- Parking uses "parked" or "verge parked"
- Location always includes full address and bracketed short address

### Person Observation — Exit Vehicle
Pattern: "[PERSON] exited Vehicle [REGO] [and ACTION]."
- Person exits vehicle using "exited Vehicle [REGO]"
- Action follows immediately after exit

### Person Observation — Entry to Vehicle
Pattern: "[PERSON] [walked from / entered] Vehicle [REGO] via the [DOOR] door."
- Entry uses "entered Vehicle [REGO] via the [driver's / passenger] door"

### Person Observation — Walking
Pattern: "[PERSON] walked [from LOCATION] [to LOCATION / across LOCATION] and [ACTION]."
- Walking uses "walked"
- Direction described with "from" and "to" or "across"

### Person Description
Pattern: "[PERSON] described as [DESCRIPTION]."
- Descriptions use "described as" not "was wearing" or "appeared to be"
- Clothing listed with colour first, then item (e.g. "black jumper", "grey track pants")
- Ends with full stop

### Out of Sight
Pattern: "...and continued out of sight [into LOCATION]."
- Always "continued out of sight" not "lost sight" or "went out of sight"
- Optional location: "into [LOCATION]"

### Photograph/Video
Pattern: "PHOTOGRAPH/S TAKEN" (standalone line, all caps, bold)
- Always on its own line
- Always in all capitals

### Route List (multi-street travel)
Pattern:
[STREET], [SUBURB],
[STREET],
...
[STREET], [SUBURB], whereat;
- Each street on its own line
- Final entry includes suburb and ends with "whereat;"
- No full stop after route list

## Grammar & Style Rules

1. **Past tense throughout** — all observations written in simple past tense
2. **Active voice** — "[PERSON] walked" not "was walked by"
3. **No abbreviations** — write out in full: "vehicle" not "veh", "registration" not "rego", "observed" not "obs", "continued" not "cont'd"
4. **Definite article before subject** — "the subject" not "subject" when referring to a previously named person generically
5. **Full stop at end of each observation paragraph** — every paragraph ends with "."
6. **Comma after time** — time entry followed by comma if sentence continues (handled by time column)
7. **Suburb in ALL CAPS** — suburb names written in all capitals (e.g. MADELEY, KENWICK, THORNLIE)
8. **Bracketed address in parentheses** — short address always in parentheses after full address on first mention of a location
9. **"and sole occupant"** — when driver is the only occupant, always "driver and sole occupant"
10. **Consistent person reference** — once a person is named, always refer to them by surname only (e.g. JEURSEN, not "the subject" or "she")
